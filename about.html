ABOUT CYBER HAT

We build the operational backbone, not just the interface.

Cyber Hat is a custom software studio. We spend more time understanding how your organization runs than we spend writing code — the code comes easily once the process is right.

our story

Started because off-the-shelf kept losing

Cyber Hat was formed around a simple observation: most organizations don't fail at software because they picked the wrong product — they fail because every off-the-shelf product assumes a workflow that isn't theirs. Schools bend their grading policy to fit software. Retailers re-train staff around a POS that doesn't match their floor. We build the other way around: the software bends to fit the organization.

Today we design and build ERP, school management, retail, healthcare, and HR systems for clients who'd rather own something built for them than rent something built for everyone.
